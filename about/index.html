I study the mechanisms underlying human sensation, perception, and cognition, and use those insights to design and develop systems that enable new forms of interaction between humans and computers or AI systems.

I currently work on interdisciplinary themes centered on Human-AI Interaction, Virtual Reality, and Brain-Computer Interface.

Across disciplines, I aim to turn outcomes from fundamental and applied research into real-world value by carrying them forward into technology development and practical deployment.

Mission

My mission is to foster cycles and interactions among research, technology development, and real-world implementation, transforming scientific knowledge into real-world value.

Vision

I aim to become someone who can pursue truth, create products and services, and deliver their value to society.
Guided by what I want to achieve, I work across disciplines, acquiring the necessary knowledge and skills to bring ideas to life.

Principles

01

Get the fundamentals right and build trust

Meaningful outcomes emerge from collaboration, and collaboration rests on mutual trust. I build that trust day by day through clear communication, timely updates, and punctuality.

02

Pursue depth and breadth of knowledge

I believe technology that creates real-world value requires both deep expertise in a core field and broad knowledge of related areas. I therefore deepen my specialization while expanding my understanding and experience across every stage, from fundamental research to real-world deployment.

03

Think deeply, prototype, and iterate

I study existing work and plan carefully, but I also begin building and testing prototypes early. I feed what I learn back into the plan, iterating toward sound results and conclusions more quickly.

04

Do not wait for opportunities—take initiative

I stay alert to what is happening around me and do not hesitate to pursue opportunities that spark my interest. Rather than simply waiting for opportunities to be offered, I take action to create the ones I need.

05

Think globally and cross boundaries

I want to turn research and development into value that improves people's lives across countries and cultures. To that end, I value collaboration with people from diverse backgrounds and use international experiences to build the foundation for understanding different perspectives and collaborating effectively across differences.

06

Stay true to my curiosity

At pivotal moments in my life, I have chosen my path based on what I genuinely want to explore rather than following others or prevailing trends. I will continue to embrace changes in my interests while staying true to the curiosity underlying them.

Interests

Human-AI Interaction

I am interested in developing AI that deeply understands users and responds flexibly to different situations. Rather than viewing AI merely as a tool, I explore relationships in which people can feel an affinity with AI, coexist with it, and evolve together.

Virtual Reality

I investigate the human sensory and perceptual mechanisms that give rise to a sense of reality and develop enabling technologies based on these insights, with the goal of creating VR systems that deliver a heightened sense of reality.

Brain-Computer Interface

I am interested in passive brain-computer interfaces (passive BCIs) that infer users' cognitive states and emotions from EEG signals and dynamically adapt the interaction based on those estimates.
